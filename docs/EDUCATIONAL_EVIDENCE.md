# Educational Evidence & Honest Limitations — Moonwood Quests

This document summarizes what **Moonwood Quests** is designed to teach, what evidence its local progress engine captures, and what claims the game intentionally avoids.

---

## 1. Ten Core Developmental Pillars Supported

1. **Confident Independent Reading (`phonics-words`)**: Prerequisite-ordered English and Spanish phonics across 6 mechanics (`sound-recognition`, `sound-blending`, `phoneme-segmentation`, `word-building`, `decoding`, `connected-reading`).
2. **Strong Foundational Mathematics (`add-within-20`, `subtract-within-20`, `unknown-number-equations`, `tens-and-ones`, `compare-lengths`, `compose-shapes`, `star-patterns`)**: Concrete-to-representational-to-abstract (CRA) progression with diagnostic misconception detection.
3. **Reading Comprehension (`reading-comprehension`)**: Original decodable and early-reader Moonwood stories (*Storykeeper Hollow*) assessing literal details, sequencing, cause/effect, and inference—with separate tracking for independent reading vs. audio narration.
4. **Vocabulary & Oral Language (`vocabulary-oral`)**: Contextual word meaning, category sorting, shades of meaning, and parent-assisted oral conversation prompts (*Creature Conversations*).
5. **Writing & Spelling (`spelling-writing`)**: Letter-tile encoding, sentence word order, capitalization/punctuation conventions, and paper-and-pencil companion prompts (*The Rune Journal*).
6. **Logical Reasoning (`logical-reasoning`)**: Clue elimination, multi-step path planning, and hypothesis testing (*The Wizard's Workshop*).
7. **Problem-Solving Strategies**: Explicit make-ten, count-on, think-addition, and balance-scale equation strategies.
8. **Curiosity & Foundational Science (`science-nature`)**: Living vs. nonliving things, animal habitats, plant survival needs, and explicitly distinguishing story magic from real-world science (*Naturekeeper Glade*).
9. **Creative Thinking (`creative-construction`)**: Constraint-based bridge, habitat, and tower design (*Moonwood Builder's Workshop*).
10. **Independent Learning & Metacognition**: Non-graded strategy reflection cards (*"What helped you solve that?"*) and calm, hint-free *Starlight Independent Checks*.

---

## 2. What Moonwood Quests Does NOT Claim

- **No IQ or "Smartest in Class" Claims**: Moonwood Quests never claims to raise IQ scores or rank a child against classmates.
- **Starting Heuristics, Not Clinical Cutoffs**: The provisional mastery threshold (`85%` accuracy across $\ge 5$ independent unseen items, $\ge 3$ task families, and $\ge 2$ representations) and spaced-review intervals (`[0, 1, 3, 7, 14]` days) are documented engineering heuristics, not psychometric norms.
- **No Unreliable Child Speech Recognition**: Oral vocabulary and open-ended writing prompts use parent/companion guidance rather than error-prone browser speech-to-text or cloud AI grading.
